General Provisions

Name, Legal Form, Head Office
CONCORDIA	Swiss	Health	and	Accident	Insurance
(CH-100.6.019.378-2),	 hereinafter	 referred	 to	 as
the	 CONCORDIA	 Association,	 is	 an	 association
whose	head	office	is	in	Lucerne.

Purpose
The	 CONCORDIA	 Association	 offers	 and	 provides
insurance	protection	against	the	financial	consequen-
ces	 of	 illness,	 maternity,	 accident,	 disability	 and
death.	It	is	a	non-profit	association.
The	CONCORDIA	Association	may	offer	insurances
itself	or	in	collaboration	with	other	insurance	carriers.
It	may	found,	acquire	an	interest	in	or	take	over	other
insurance	carriers.
The	 CONCORDIA	 Association	 supports	 efforts	 to
prevent	illnesses	and	accidents	as	well	as	disability
and	premature	death.

Area of Activity
The	area	of	activity	of	the	CONCORDIA	Association
covers	 Switzerland,	 its	 external	 border	 areas	 and
the	Principality	of	Liechtenstein.

Notices
General	notices	of	the	CONCORDIA	Association	are
published	in	circulars	or	in	the	members’	magazine
and	are	legally	binding.	The	members’	magazine	is
the	customer	magazine	of	CONCORDIA	Swiss	Health
and	Accident	Insurance	Ltd	and	CONCORDIA	Insur-
ances	Ltd.

Commencement of Membership
Any	natural	person	living	in	the	area	of	activity	who
takes	out	a	mandatory	health	care	insurance	or	a

CONCORDIA	 Association	 have	 the	 right	 to	 vote	 as
well	as	the	right	to	elect	and	to	be	elected	in	their

CONCORDIA	Association.
