Taxes: the employer deducts taxes directly from the wages of workers with a B residence permit and transfers them to the tax authorities.



Thanks to the free movement of people in the EU and EFTA, you may enter Switzerland and live and work here. To do so, you will need a valid contract of employ- ment.



Within a period of 14 days of arrival, and before taking up your position, you must obtain a residence permit from the municipal residents’ service of the town in question.

Take the following with you: your identity document, writ- ten declaration of employment by the employer

Professional pension plan (BVG). Everybody who has retirement insurance must contribute. This money will be paid out to you at the end of your working life in Switzer- land, or when you retire.

Workers are entitled to a short- term residence permit (L EU/ EFTA), which is limited to the duration of the contract on the employment certificate.

Retirement insurance (AHV) is mandatory in Switzerland and is deducted directly from your salary.



Employment relationship from one to five years

On presenting an employment certificate that indicates the rele- vant duration, workers will receive a B EU/EFTA residence permit valid for five years.



Mandatory basic insurance receipt (can be submitted up to 3 months after entry date)

Take out home insurance and civil liability insurance. These insurance policies cover any damage to your home or any damage you cause to third parties.



Self-employment

EU/EFTA citizens entering Switzerland to work as self- employed persons will initially receive a B EU/EFTA residence permit for a period of five years, provided they can prove that they are actually working as self-em- ployed persons at the time of submitting their application.
